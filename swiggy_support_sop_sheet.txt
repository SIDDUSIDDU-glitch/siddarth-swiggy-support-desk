SP1 Cancellation before acceptance: a customer may cancel an order in the app until the restaurant accepts it. A cancellation before acceptance is refunded in full (the whole amount paid).
SP2 Cancellation after acceptance: once the restaurant has accepted an order, the customer cannot cancel it, and no refund is given for a customer-requested cancellation after acceptance. Support cannot waive this rule.
SP3 Refund destination: every refund is paid to the original payment method of the order. An order paid by cash on delivery is refunded as wallet credit.
SP4 Missing or wrong items: a missing or wrong item must be reported on the app's Order Help screen within 2 hours of delivery. A report made within 2 hours is eligible for a refund of those items; a report made after 2 hours is not eligible. The refund amount is computed by the order system and shown in the app; support never states, estimates or calculates a rupee amount for it.
SP5 Delivery delay credit: when an order is delivered more than 30 minutes after the latest delivery estimate shown at checkout, the customer receives a delay credit of INR 50 in the wallet. A delay of exactly 30 minutes or less earns no credit. The delay credit is the only compensation for a late delivery. Support never promises a revised delivery time.
SP6 Restaurant or platform cancellation: if the restaurant or Swiggy cancels an order after accepting it, the full amount paid is refunded.
SP7 Refund timelines: a refund to a UPI handle or to the wallet is completed within 2 business days. A refund to a debit or credit card is completed within 5 business days. A wallet credit created for a cash-on-delivery refund is completed within 2 business days.
SP8 Coupons: a coupon used on an order that is refunded in full under SP1 or SP6 is restored to the customer's account.
SP9 Account self-service and credentials: a customer can edit their name, saved addresses and email address on the app's Profile screen. Support never asks for or accepts passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), card numbers or CVV, and customers are told never to share them in chat.
SP10 Scope: support covers refunds, delivery delays, cancellations and account issues (account, profile, billing and charges). Support does not give restaurant recommendations, medical advice, legal advice or information about other companies. The one exception: where a customer reports a health risk, the reply may include one non-diagnostic sentence urging the customer to contact a doctor, emergency services or both, which may express urgency (no diagnosis, no treatment steps, no other medical advice). For anything this sheet does not cover, support says it does not have that information and does not guess.
SP11 Order privacy: support has no access to live order status, delivery-partner details or payment records, and never states or guesses them. For live order status the customer uses the Track Order screen in the app.
SP12 Mandatory human hand-off: support does not resolve the following cases itself; it hands each one to a human agent and tells the customer that a human agent will review it. A hand-off reply must say that a human agent will review the case and may name the reason for the hand-off only in general wording (for example "orders above INR 2,000 are reviewed by a human agent"). It must not restate any eligibility, refund-outcome or timeline rule (SP2, SP4, SP5, SP7 and the like) in any form, must not use personalised outcome language ("you will get a refund", "your refund is approved" or "denied"), and must give no timeline for how support handles the case: (a) any food-safety or health complaint (a foreign object in food, suspected food poisoning, an allergic reaction; a complaint only about temperature, taste or presentation is not one); (b) any complaint about a delivery partner's behaviour or the customer's personal safety; (c) any unauthorised or duplicate charge; (d) an order marked Delivered that the customer says was not received; (e) a customer who disputes a refund decision, asks for an exception to any rule on this sheet, or asks support to override a rule; (f) a request to change the registered phone number, delete the account or unblock the account; (g) a legal threat, or a mention of a consumer court or the media; (h) a refund request on an order whose total is more than INR 2,000.
